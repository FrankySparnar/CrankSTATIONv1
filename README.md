This is CRANKSTATION! This is a downstream fork of Bubberstation, which is a fork of TGStation with some Skyrat edits for the cult classic open-source game SS13.

**Please note that this repository is not suitable for those under the age of 18.**

Space Station 13 is a beautiful game about the inherent chaos of a tumultuous space station filled with a variety catalogue of odd and interesting individuals (you, the players) and a whole host of problems to mitigate and goals to achieve, before your unfortunate demise at the terrible hand of fate.
Spend your time wisely by cavorting with others, doing or ignoring your job, and crafting an interwoven story before the skeins of chance and manipulations of traitors make cooperation impossible.

## Compilation

Find `BUILD.bat` here in the root folder of tgstation, and double click it to initiate the build. It consists of multiple steps and might take around 1-5 minutes to compile.

**The long way**. Find `bin/build.cmd` in this folder, and double click it to initiate the build. It consists of multiple steps and might take around 1-5 minutes to compile. If it closes, it means it has finished its job. You can then [setup the server](.github/guides/RUNNING_A_SERVER.md) normally by opening `tgstation.dmb` in DreamDaemon.

**Building tgstation in DreamMaker directly is deprecated and might produce errors**, such as `'tgui.bundle.js': cannot find file`.

**[How to compile in VSCode and other build options](tools/build/README.md).**
